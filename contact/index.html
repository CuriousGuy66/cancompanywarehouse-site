Worth knowing before you ask

  • There is no kitchen, no bar and no event staff, so no catering minimum either. Bring your own caterer.
  • Rates are published. See the whole card rather than asking us for a quote sheet.
  • Events, production days and anything with alcohol need a certificate of insurance naming KNI Investments LLC. Meetings and workshops do not. The detail is on the pricing page.
  • Amplified music comes down at 10pm. Roanoke city regulation, not ours.
  • If the room is wrong for what you’re running, we’ll tell you that instead of selling you the date.

Getting here

315 Albemarle Ave SE

The building is the long brick one with S. H. Heironimus Co. Warehouse painted on its side.

From Williamson Road: turn onto Albemarle Ave (left from downtown, right from South Roanoke), go under the highway and over the railroad tracks, then take an immediate right onto the small side street.
From 4th Street: turn right onto Albemarle Ave and, before the tracks and the highway, turn left onto the small side street.

Park free in the U-shaped lot. Tours meet at the glass door marked 315, between the two buildings. For a booking, follow the walkway along the long building to the green door marked 315; we send a door code before your date, and the same code opens every door on the way. Take the elevator or the stairs to the second floor and follow the path to the double doors with our sign.

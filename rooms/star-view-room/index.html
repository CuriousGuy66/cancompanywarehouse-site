The room as it stands

Daylight is the thing you’re booking.

Glazing runs along three walls, with skylights over the north-center, so the room is lit without the overheads on for most of the working day. The floors are the original plank. The brick is the original brick. Nothing has been dressed up to look old, because it isn’t.

Why it’s called that

The Star sits in the south windows.

After dark the Mill Mountain Star comes up on the hillside directly in line with the south glass, over the brick parapet of the building across the yard, the one still carrying the Continental Can ghost sign. You can see both from inside the room without moving.

It is the best thing about a 5pm booking in November, and it is the reason the room has the name it has.
